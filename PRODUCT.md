# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mandoob serves UAE PRO firms operating business-registration and compliance workflows, plus their customers and employees. The primary authenticated dashboard user is a PRO firm owner managing many client companies, documents, registrations, renewals, payments, and deadlines under time pressure.

## Product Purpose

Mandoob centralizes secure, tenant-isolated PRO operations. Success means users can identify the next required action, complete it through a clear workflow, and trust that client data, documents, permissions, deadlines, and audit records remain accurate.

## Brand Personality

Operational, warm, and precise. The product should feel expert and calm under pressure, with restrained moments of Mandoob orange energy rather than decorative spectacle.

## Anti-references

- Generic white-card SaaS grids with weak hierarchy.
- Decorative gradients, glass effects, fake metrics, or ornamental charts.
- Consumer-playful controls, neon cyber styling, and dense enterprise UI without clear priority.
- Unfamiliar interactions that slow routine operational work.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the next decision or action.
- Earn trust through real data, explicit states, and predictable controls.
- Keep operational density readable through clear hierarchy and purposeful spacing.
- Reuse one coherent dashboard vocabulary across roles and workflows.
- Make partial failures and empty states useful rather than silent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, color-independent status cues, English and Arabic localization, natural RTL layout, explicit locale formatting, light and dark themes, and mobile use down to 390 pixels without page overflow.
